# Chicken Fajita Rice Bake

## Ingredients
* Fajita spice mix: 2 packages (Santa Maria, 28 g)
* Chicken broth: 5 dl
* Olive oil: 2 tbsp
* Chicken: 800 g
* Rice: 5 dl
* Red bell pepper: 1
* Green bell pepper: 1
* Onion: ½

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C
2. Dice the bell peppers and onion, slice the chicken and place everything in a bowl
3. Add the oil and the seasoning to the bowl and mix until the seasoning is evenly distributed
4. In a casserole dish, add the rice and the chicken broth, then add the vegetables and chicken
5. Cover the dish with aluminum foil and bake in the middle of the oven for about 1 hour (until the rice is fully cooked)

## Serving suggestions
* Serve in tortilla breads with some feta cheese and a sauce/dressing of your choice
* Or eat it as it is with some tzatziki
