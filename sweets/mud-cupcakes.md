# Mud Cupcakes

## Ingredients
* Butter: 100 g
* Eggs: 2
* Sugar: 2.5 dl
* Vanilla sugar: 2 tsp
* Flour: 2 dl
* Salt: 1 pinch
* Baking powder: 0.5 tsp
* Cacao: 4 tbsp

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 200 °C
2. Crack the eggs into a bowl
3. Mix the dry ingredients in a separate vessel
4. Mix them into the eggs along with the melted butter, then stir until smooth
5. Add to cupcake molds and bake in the middle of the oven for 8-10 minutes

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with even more cupcakes
