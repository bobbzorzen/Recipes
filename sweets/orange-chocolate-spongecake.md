# Spongecake with Orange and Chocolate

Original recipe: https://zeinaskitchen.se/sockerkaka-med-apelsin-och-choklad

## Ingredients
* Butter: 125 g
* Eggs: 3
* Sugar: 2 dl
* Flour: 3 dl
* Baking soda: 2 tsp
* Vanilla sugar: 1 tsp
* Orange: 1 whole
* Milk chocolate: 300 g
* Coconut flakes: enough to "bread" the pan

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 175 °C
2. Butter and "bread" the pan (with coconut flakes)
3. Melt the butter
4. Grate the zest off the orange into the butter
5. Squeeze the orange and add the juice to the butter
6. Mix thoroughly and let cool
7. Mix the flour, baking soda and vanilla sugar in a bowl
8. Chop the chocolate into 0.5-1 cm large chunks
9. Whisk the eggs and sugar until white and puffy
10. Sieve and fold in the flour mix
11. Gently fold in the butter mix as well as 2/3 of the chocolate
12. Add the mix to the pan
13. Sprinkle the rest of the chocolate on top for a better spread in the cake
14. Bake in the middle of the oven for approximately 25 minutes (until the cake has set and isn't gooey in the middle)
15. Let the cake cool in the pan for a while before flipping it out

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with tea
