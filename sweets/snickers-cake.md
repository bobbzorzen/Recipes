# Snickers Cake (Snickerskaka)

## Ingredients
* Peanut butter (smooth): 1 jar (400 g)
* Sugar: 1 dl
* Vanilla sugar: 1 tsp
* Baking syrup (light): 2 dl
* Rice Krispies: 1 liter
* Milk chocolate (regular bars, not baking chocolate): 300 g
* Dark chocolate (regular bars, not baking chocolate): 100 g

## Instructions
1. Put the peanut butter, sugar and syrup in a cooking pot
2. Heat it while stirring thoroughly until it's smooth and hot. ***Take care not to burn the sugar***
3. Remove from the heat and gently fold in the Rice Krispies thoroughly
4. Spread on a baking sheet (covered by baking or wax paper)
5. Melt all the chocolate in a water bath (to avoid direct heat that can burn the chocolate)
6. Spread the chocolate over the peanut butter/crispy batter
7. Cool in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before cutting into bite-sized pieces

## Serving suggestions
* Goes great with tea!
* Or really any warm beverage that helps melt the cake in your mouth
