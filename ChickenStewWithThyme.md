# Chicken Stew with Thyme and Haricots Verts

## Ingredients
* Potatoes: 900 g
* Haricots verts: 300 g
* Chicken fillets: 4
* Cooking cream: 2½ dl
* Fresh thyme: ½ tsp
* Milk: 1½ dl
* Corn starch: 1 tbsp
* Soy sauce (Japanese): 1 tbsp
* Salt
* Pepper

## Instructions
1. Cut the potatoes in half and boil them
2. Slice the chicken into small bits
3. Fry the chicken with all the spices
4. Mix the starch and the milk together
5. Add the starch/milk mix and the soy sauce
6. Add salt, pepper and soy sauce to your taste
