# Garlic Butter Shrimp Pasta

## Ingredients
* Fettuccine pasta (or whatever pasta you like): 230 g
* Shrimp: 500 g
* Salt
* Pepper
* Butter: 8 tbsp (2 tbsp + 6 tbsp)
* Garlic: 4 cloves
* Oregano: ½ tsp
* Crushed red pepper: ½ tsp
* Arugula: 5 dl
* Grated Parmesan cheese: 0.5 dl
* Fresh parsley (chopped): 2 tbsp (optional)

## Instructions
1. Cook the pasta in a large pot of boiling salted water according to the package instructions, then drain well
2. Season the shrimp with salt and pepper, to taste, and set aside
3. Melt 2 tbsp of the butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the garlic, oregano and red pepper flakes and cook, stirring frequently, until fragrant, about 1-2 minutes
4. Add the shrimp and cook, stirring occasionally, until pink, about 2-3 minutes. Set aside
5. Melt the remaining 6 tbsp butter in the skillet. Stir in the pasta, arugula and Parmesan until the arugula begins to wilt, about 2 minutes
6. Stir in the shrimp

## Serving suggestions
* Serve immediately, garnished with parsley if desired
