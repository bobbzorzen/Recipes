# Pork Stew

## Ingredients
* Pork fillet: 500 g
* Calf broth: 1 tbsp
* Onion: 1
* Chili sauce: 2 tbsp
* Curry: 1 tsp
* Paprika powder: 1 tsp
* Oregano: 2 tsp
* Water: 2.5 dl
* Salt
* Black pepper
* Butter

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C
2. Slice the pork into small bite-sized bits
3. Salt and pepper the pork to your taste
4. Fry the pork in rounds using butter, rinsing the pan with the water in between and after the rounds
5. Place the pork in a casserole dish
6. Chop the onion and fry it gently together with the curry, paprika and oregano
7. Pour the onions, fry water, chili sauce and broth into the dish and mix
8. Finish the dish in the oven for 10 minutes

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with boiled or fried potatoes
