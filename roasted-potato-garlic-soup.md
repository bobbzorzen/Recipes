# Roasted Potato & Garlic Soup

## Ingredients
* Potatoes: 1 kg
* Shallots: 2
* Garlic: 5 heads
* Dried thyme: 1 tsp
* Chicken or vegetable broth: 7.5 dl
* Heavy cream: 2.5 dl
* Olive oil for drizzling
* Salt and pepper to taste

### Toppings
* Crispy fried bacon: 2 packages
* Scallions (diced)
* Grated mild-ish cheese

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C
2. Peel and dice the potatoes, peel and halve the shallots and cut off the tops of the garlic heads
3. Place the potatoes, shallots and garlic on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper
4. Drizzle with olive oil and season with salt, pepper and thyme
5. Roast in the oven for 30 minutes, take out and cover with aluminum foil, then put back in the oven for 15-25 minutes (until the potatoes are tender)
6. Transfer the veggies to a large pot and squeeze the garlic out of its skin
7. Add the broth and the cream to the pot
8. Blend with an immersion blender until smooth and creamy
9. Add salt and pepper to taste
10. Prepare the toppings (bacon, scallions and cheese)

## Serving suggestions
* Ladle into bowls
* Top generously with crispy fried bacon and sprinkle some scallions and cheese on top
* Some plain white bread goes great with this
