# Mocha Squares (Kärleksmums)

## Ingredients
### Cake
* Butter: 225 g
* Milk: 2 dl
* Eggs: 5
* Granulated sugar: 4½ dl
* All-purpose flour: 6 dl
* Vanilla sugar: 1½ tbsp
* Cacao: 1¼ dl
* Baking powder: 3 tsp

### Topping
* Butter: 75 g
* Strong coffee: ¾ dl
* Cacao: ½ dl
* Vanilla sugar: 1½ tbsp
* Icing sugar: 5 dl

### Garnish
* Coconut flakes

## Instructions
### Cake
1. Heat the oven to 175 °C
2. Butter and "bread" a baking dish with a high edge, 25x30 cm (or use a baking sheet)
3. Melt the butter, pour in the milk and let cool
4. Whisk the eggs and sugar until puffy, preferably with a machine (depending on how puffy you want it, you'll have to whisk more or less)
5. Sieve the flour, vanilla sugar, cacao and baking powder down into the egg/sugar mixture
6. Add the butter/milk mixture and mix to a smooth batter
7. Pour the batter into the dish
8. Bake in the middle of the oven for about 20 minutes

### Topping (make while the cake is baking)
1. Melt the butter in a pot
2. Add the coffee, cacao, vanilla sugar and icing sugar. Mix well
3. Take the cake out of the oven and let it cool for a while
4. Spread the icing over the warm cake and sprinkle an unbelievably huge amount of coconut flakes on top
