# Oatmeal Chocolate Cookies

## Ingredients
* Butter (room temperature, rumstempererat): 200g
* Sugar: 2.5 dl
* Vanilla sugar: 1 tbsp
* Salt: 1 ml (1 krm)
* Flour: 2 dl
* Baking soda: 2 tsp
* Rolled oats: 4.5 dl
* Dark chocolate (chopped): 2 dl

## Cooking:
* Heat oven to 180°
* Mix butter, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt until soft
* Mix the flour with the baking soda
* Mix the flour mix into the sweet butter
* Mix in the rolled oats and the chopped chocolate
* Shape into 30 balls in total and spread them over two baking sheets (covered by baking paper)
* Bake in the middle of the oven for approximately 15-16m (they are done when they have spread out and turned golden brown)
* Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet until they have set

## Serving suggestions
 * Serve with a glass of milk
