# Oatmeal Chocolate Cookies

* Yield: 30 cookies

## Ingredients
* Butter (room temperature, rumstempererat): 200 g
* Sugar: 2.5 dl
* Vanilla sugar: 1 tbsp
* Salt: 1 ml (1 krm)
* Flour: 2 dl
* Baking soda: 2 tsp
* Rolled oats: 4.5 dl
* Dark chocolate (chopped): 2 dl

## Instructions
1. Heat the oven to 180 °C
2. Mix the butter, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt until soft
3. Mix the flour with the baking soda
4. Mix the flour mix into the sweet butter
5. Mix in the rolled oats and the chopped chocolate
6. Shape into 30 balls in total and spread them over two baking sheets (covered by baking paper)
7. Bake in the middle of the oven for approximately 15-16 minutes (they are done when they have spread out and turned golden brown)
8. Let the cookies cool on the baking sheet until they have set

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with a glass of milk
