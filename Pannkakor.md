# Pancakes (Pannkakor)

* Yield: 1 portion, about 4-5 pancakes
* Each pancake is about 1.5 dl batter

## Ingredients
* Wheat flour: 2 dl
* Eggs: 2
* Milk: 4 dl
* Salt: 0.5 ml (0.5 krm)
* Vanilla sugar: 1 ml (1 krm)
* Butter for frying

## Instructions
1. Mix the flour, salt and vanilla sugar in a bowl
2. Mix in half of the milk and work out any lumps
3. Mix in the eggs and the remaining milk
4. Fry each pancake (about 1.5 dl batter) in plenty of butter

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with jam, sugar or Nutella
* You can make it fancier with a little cream
