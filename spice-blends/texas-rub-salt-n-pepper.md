# Texas Rub, Salt 'n Pepper

## Ingredients
* Sweet paprika: 2 tsp
* Black pepper: 1 tsp
* Onion: 1 tsp
* Garlic: 2 tsp
* Chili pepper: 1 tsp
* Cayenne pepper: 1 tsp
* Coriander/cilantro: 1 tsp
* Salt: 1 tsp
* Sugar: 0.5 tsp (optional)

## Instructions
1. Mix the spices, add and remove to your liking
