# Cajun Chicken Pasta

## Ingredients
* Linguine pasta: 110 g
* Skinless, boneless chicken breast halves: 2
* Cajun seasoning: 2 tsp
* Butter: 2 tbsp
* Red bell pepper (sliced): 1
* Green bell pepper (sliced): 1
* Fresh mushrooms (sliced): 4
* Green onion (chopped): 1
* Heavy cream: 2.4 dl
* Dried basil: ¼ tsp
* Lemon pepper: ¼ tsp
* Salt: ¼ tsp
* Garlic powder: ⅛ tsp
* Ground black pepper: ⅛ tsp
* Grated Parmesan cheese: 0.6 dl

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Add the pasta and cook for 8-10 minutes or until al dente, then drain
2. Place the chicken and the Cajun seasoning in a plastic bag and shake to coat
3. In a large skillet over medium heat, sauté the chicken in butter (or margarine) until almost tender (5-7 minutes)
4. Add the bell peppers, mushrooms and green onion. Sauté and stir for 2-3 minutes, then reduce the heat
5. Add the cream, basil, lemon pepper, salt, garlic powder and ground black pepper. Heat through
6. Add the cooked linguine, toss and heat through
7. Sprinkle with grated Parmesan cheese and serve
