# Blackcurrant Cordial with Currant Leaves (Svartvinbärssaft med vinbärsblad)

A classic recipe for homemade blackcurrant cordial where the leaves give extra flavour and aroma.

## Ingredients
* Blackcurrants: 2 liters
* Blackcurrant leaves: 2 liters
* Water: 4 liters
* Granulated sugar: 2 kg
* Citric acid: 2 tsp
* Sodium benzoate (preservative): just under 1 ml (1 krm) per liter of finished cordial

## Cooking:
* Put the blackcurrants and the leaves in a large pot
* Pour over the water and boil for 20m
* Strain the juice through a sieve or straining cloth (preferably as soon as possible after boiling)
* Pour the strained juice back into the cleaned pot
* Add the sugar and the citric acid
* Bring to a boil and simmer for approximately 5m, until the sugar has dissolved
* Remove the pot from the heat and stir in just under 1 ml (1 krm) sodium benzoate per liter of finished cordial
* Pour the hot cordial into well cleaned, preferably sterilized, bottles and seal them immediately

## Yield
* Approximately 7 bottles (depending on bottle size)
