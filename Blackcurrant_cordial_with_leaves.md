# Blackcurrant Cordial with Currant Leaves (Svartvinbärssaft med vinbärsblad)

A classic recipe for homemade blackcurrant cordial where the leaves give extra flavour and aroma.

* Yield: approximately 7 bottles (depending on bottle size)

## Ingredients
* Blackcurrants: 2 liters
* Blackcurrant leaves: 2 liters
* Water: 4 liters
* Granulated sugar: 2 kg
* Citric acid: 2 tsp
* Sodium benzoate (preservative): just under 1 ml (1 krm) per liter of finished cordial

## Instructions
1. Put the blackcurrants and the leaves in a large pot
2. Pour over the water and boil for 20 minutes
3. Strain the juice through a sieve or straining cloth (preferably as soon as possible after boiling)
4. Pour the strained juice back into the cleaned pot
5. Add the sugar and the citric acid
6. Bring to a boil and simmer for approximately 5 minutes, until the sugar has dissolved
7. Remove the pot from the heat and stir in just under 1 ml (1 krm) sodium benzoate per liter of finished cordial
8. Pour the hot cordial into well cleaned, preferably sterilized, bottles and seal them immediately
