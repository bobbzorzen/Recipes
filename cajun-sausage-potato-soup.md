# Cajun Sausage & Potato Soup

## Ingredients
### Sausage
* Vegetable oil: 1 tbsp
* Andouille sausage (sliced into 0.5 cm rounds): 380 g

### Vegetables
* Large onion (diced): 1 (about 2.4 dl)
* Celery (diced): 1.2 dl (about 1 rib)
* Red bell pepper (seeded, diced): ½
* Garlic (minced): 2 tsp

### Soup
* Cajun seasoning: 1 tsp
* Kosher salt: ½ tsp
* Black pepper: ½ tsp
* Paprika: ½ tsp
* Cayenne pepper: ¼ tsp
* Chicken broth: 1L
* Large russet potatoes (peeled, cubed): 4
* Heavy whipping cream: 1.5 dl
* Mild cheddar cheese (shredded): 2.5 dl
* Parsley (chopped, for garnish)

## Instructions
### Sausage
1. Add the oil to a large pot over medium heat. Once hot, add the sausage and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 3-4 minutes
2. Remove the sausage from the pot and set aside on a plate or in a bowl while you prepare the rest of the soup

### Vegetables
1. To the same pot over medium heat, add the onion, celery and bell pepper. Cook until softened, about 5-8 minutes
2. Add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute

### Soup
1. To the pot, add the Cajun seasoning, salt, pepper, paprika, cayenne pepper, chicken broth and potatoes
2. Reduce the heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes, or until the potatoes are fork-tender
3. Return the sausage to the pot and add the heavy cream and shredded cheese
4. Let the soup simmer for an additional 5 minutes, or until everything is heated through and the cheese is melted

## Serving suggestions
* Garnish with parsley
* Serve warm
