# Chicken Stroganoff with Rice

## Ingredients
* Rice: enough for 4 people
* Onion: 1
* Garlic: 2 cloves
* Chicken: 600 g
* Paprika powder: 2 tsp
* Tomato purée: 3 tbsp
* Vegetable broth: 5 dl
* Crème fraîche: 3 dl
* Salt
* Pepper

## Instructions
1. Boil the rice
2. Chop the onion and garlic, fry them, then set them aside
3. Season the chicken with salt and pepper and fry it
4. Stir in the paprika, tomato purée, broth, onion, garlic and crème fraîche
5. Let it simmer for 10 minutes
