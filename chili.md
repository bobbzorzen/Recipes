# Chuck Chili

## Ingredients
* Chuck: 1 kg
* Onions: 2
* Garlic: 8 cloves
* Red bell pepper: 1
* Dried chipotle chilies: 3 (can be replaced by some smoked paprika powder for a milder chili)
* Black beans: 800 g
* Crushed tomatoes: 800 g
* Tomato purée: 3-4 tbsp
* Cumin: 1 tbsp
* Paprika powder: 1 tbsp
* Dried oregano: 1 tbsp
* Bay leaves: 2
* Meat or vegetable broth: 2 tbsp (or 2 bouillon cubes)
* Salt and pepper to taste
* Canola oil for frying

## Instructions
1. Soak the chipotles in lukewarm water for approximately 30 minutes
2. Purée the chipotles with 2 tbsp of the water they were soaked in
3. Chop the meat, onions and bell pepper into small cubes
4. Crush the garlic
5. Heat a pan with canola oil and fry the meat until it's properly colored
6. Add the onions, bell pepper and garlic and fry while stirring for approximately 4-5 minutes
7. Add the oregano, cumin, chipotle purée, paprika powder and tomato purée and fry while stirring for a few more minutes
8. Add the crushed tomatoes, bay leaves, broth/bouillon, water and salt & pepper
9. Heat the chili to a boil, then lower the heat and let it simmer for about 2-3 hours (add water if the chili seems dry)
10. Remember to taste and adjust the spices if needed
11. Gently fold in the rinsed beans and let it boil for another 20-30 minutes
12. Done, it should be ready to serve

## Serving suggestions
* Tortilla chips
* Shredded cheddar cheese
* Sour cream or crème fraîche
* Pickled jalapeños
* Lime
* Absolutely not under any circumstances should you add cilantro or coriander to anything ever
