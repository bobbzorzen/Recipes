# Waffles

* Time: 30 minutes
* Yield: 6-10 waffles

## Ingredients
* Butter: 100 g
* Wheat flour: 3 dl
* Milk: 4 dl
* Baking powder: 1 tsp
* Salt: 0.25 tsp

## Instructions
1. Mix the flour, baking powder and salt in a bowl
2. Whisk in half of the milk until it's smooth
3. Add the remaining milk as well as the butter (melted and cooled)
4. Cook in a waffle iron brushed with melted butter or oil

## Serving suggestions
* Serve with jam and cream, perhaps some fresh fruit or Nutella
